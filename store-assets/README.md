# Firefox Add-ons listing graphics

Upload `icon-128.png` as the custom add-on icon. Upload the three 1280 × 800 PNG screenshots in filename order. These are unannotated captures of the extension's built-in interactive preview; the visible banner identifies the prices and history as samples. No personal watchlist or account data was used.

Suggested German screenshot descriptions:

1. **Alles im Blick:** Wunschliste, beobachtete Preisverläufe und Zielpreise an einem Ort. Beispielansicht.
2. **In Sekunden startklar:** Produktlink einfügen, Wunschpreis festlegen und bei Bedarf die Preisquelle anpassen. Beispielansicht.
3. **Preise verstehen:** Verlauf, eigener beobachteter 30-Tage-Tiefstpreis und Zielpreis im Detail. Beispielansicht; kein offizieller Händler-Referenzpreis.

Suggested English screenshot descriptions:

1. **Your watchlist at a glance:** Track observed price history and target prices in one place. Sample preview.
2. **Start in seconds:** Add a product link, set a target, and customize the price source when needed. Sample preview.
3. **Know the price story:** See observed history, your own 30-day low, and the target in one view. Sample preview; not the retailer's official reference price.

The wordless icon pairs a lantern silhouette with a falling price line, and remains legible at 16 and 32 px. The icon source is `icon.svg`; `icon-128.png` is the raster image for AMO. The extension package also uses this icon starting with version 0.2.3. Mozilla recommends a recognizable icon without words and a short screenshot sequence that shows key features. Source: https://extensionworkshop.com/documentation/develop/create-an-appealing-listing/
